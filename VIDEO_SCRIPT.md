## 🎬 Section 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)

### [Visual]:
- Fast cinematic close-up of ChopSamp's dark tactile hardware interface inside a DAW.
- Hands on a MIDI keyboard / pads chopping and triggering a soulful vinyl sample with glowing green LED feedback.
- Quick cut to dropping a MIDI sequence straight into the playlist with one drag.

### [Voiceover]:

## 🎬 Section 2: Loading & Zero-Crossing Slicing (0:30 - 1:15)

### [Visual]:
- Dragging a `.wav` file into ChopSamp.
- Waveform draws instantly with sample metadata in the bottom LCD strip (`44.1kHz`, `16bit`, duration).
- Showing the **Slice Mode** dropdown: Selecting **Slice Grid (1/4 Note)** and hitting the **SLICE** button.
- Showing **Slice Transients** with sensitivity slider adjusting markers in real-time.
- Demonstrating manual marker tweaks: Double-clicking to add/remove markers, zooming in with the scroll wheel, and dragging the top handle `↔` snapping seamlessly to zero crossings.

### [Voiceover]:
> "Loading samples is effortless — just drag and drop any audio file right into the interface. 
> With multiple slicing engines, you can chop by musical Grid divisions, detect dynamic Transients, or go fully experimental with Random slicing.
> Want total precision? Double-click anywhere to add or remove markers, zoom with your mouse wheel, and drag marker handles — every chop automatically snaps to zero crossings so you never get clicks or pops."

---

## 🎬 Section 3: Sound Sculpting & Voice Choking (1:15 - 2:00)

### [Visual]:
- Close-up of left controls: adjusting **Pitch** (+2 semitones), **HPF** to cut bass, **LPF** to darken tone.
- Flipping on the **REV** (Reverse) button on a single slice to demonstrate per-slice reverse playback.
- Switching between **White Keys Only** and **Chromatic** key mapping.
- Jamming a fast beat showing **Mono Choke** mode cutting off slices cleanly with smooth **XFade** crossfading.

### [Voiceover]:
> "Every slice has its own dedicated sound engine. Tweak pitch across 4 octaves, sculpt frequencies with high-pass and low-pass filters, adjust stereo panning, and reverse individual chops on the fly.
> With **Mono Choke** mode, new slices instantly choke the previous one with adjustable anti-click crossfading — giving you that tight, rhythmic hip-hop bounce.
> And with **White Keys Only** mode, your keyboard turns into an MPC pad layout — no awkward finger gymnastics over black keys."

## 🎬 Section 4: ADSR Visualizer & 20 Sample Matrix (2:00 - 2:45)

### [Visual]:
- Tweaking Attack, Decay, Sustain, and Release rotary knobs while the oscilloscope visualizer renders the curve dynamically.
- Clicking the **APPLY TO ALL** button to copy envelope settings to all slices.
- Clicking through the **20 Sample Matrix Tabs (1 to 20)**, showing a completely different sample loaded on tab 2 and tab 3.

### [Voiceover]:
> "Shape your envelopes with the real-time ADSR oscilloscope visualizer. Once you dial in the perfect punchy attack or smooth release, hit **Apply to All** to copy it across all your slices in one click.
> Need more sounds? ChopSamp gives you **20 independent sample slots**. Load an entire album's worth of chops, drum kits, and vocal stabs in a single instance."

---

## 🎬 Section 5: The Game Changer — Drag MIDI & Drag Audio (2:45 - 3:30)

### [Visual]:
- Cursor clicks and drags the **DRAG MIDI** button straight onto the DAW track. The full sequenced MIDI pattern appears on the DAW timeline.
- Hitting DAW Play: the timeline triggers ChopSamp perfectly.
- Cursor clicks and drags the **DRAG AUDIO** button: drops the isolated slice WAV file directly into the DAW playlist.

### [Voiceover]:
> "And here is the ultimate workflow unlock: **Drag MIDI** and **Drag Audio**.
> Grab the Drag MIDI button, drop it onto your track, and boom — your entire sequenced slice pattern is right on your DAW timeline.
> Want to export a single isolated audio chop into your arrangement? Just drag the Drag Audio button directly into your playlist."
